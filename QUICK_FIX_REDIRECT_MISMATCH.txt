================================================================================
        QUICK FIX: Error 400 - redirect_uri_mismatch
================================================================================

ERROR MESSAGE:
"Access blocked: This app's request is invalid"
"Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch"

THIS MEANS:
Google OAuth doesn't recognize your Render backend URL as authorized.

================================================================================
IMMEDIATE FIX (5 MINUTES)
================================================================================

STEP 1: FIND YOUR RENDER BACKEND URL
================================================================================

1. Go to: https://dashboard.render.com
2. Click on your backend service
3. Look at the top - you'll see a URL like:
   https://XXXXXXXX.onrender.com

   Example: https://student-registration-backend.onrender.com

4. COPY THIS EXACT URL (we'll call it YOUR_BACKEND_URL)

================================================================================
STEP 2: ADD URL TO GOOGLE OAUTH CONSOLE
================================================================================

1. Go to: https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials

2. Click on "OAuth 2.0 Client IDs"

3. Click on your client ID:
   Client ID: 130550221013-2hcgtmor7c20v309f6p60a6e3ck2sosj.apps.googleusercontent.com

4. Scroll down to "Authorized redirect URIs"

5. Click "+ ADD URI"

6. Paste EXACTLY (replace with your actual Render URL):
   https://YOUR_BACKEND_URL/auth/google/callback

   Example:
   https://student-registration-backend.onrender.com/auth/google/callback

   ⚠️ IMPORTANT:
   - Must start with https:// (not http://)
   - Must end with /auth/google/callback
   - NO trailing slash after callback
   - NO spaces before or after

7. Click "SAVE" at the bottom of the page

8. ⏰ WAIT 5-10 MINUTES for Google to apply changes

================================================================================
STEP 3: UPDATE RENDER ENVIRONMENT VARIABLE
================================================================================

1. Go to: https://dashboard.render.com

2. Click on your backend service

3. Click "Environment" in left sidebar

4. Find or Add: GOOGLE_CALLBACK_URL

5. Set the value to (replace with your actual URL):
   https://YOUR_BACKEND_URL/auth/google/callback

   Example:
   https://student-registration-backend.onrender.com/auth/google/callback

6. Click "Save Changes"

7. Render will auto-redeploy (wait 2-3 minutes)

================================================================================
STEP 4: ALSO ADD FRONTEND URL
================================================================================

While you're in Google Console, also add:

1. Under "Authorized JavaScript origins", click "+ ADD URI"

2. Add your Vercel frontend URL:
   https://your-frontend-app.vercel.app

3. Also add your backend URL:
   https://YOUR_BACKEND_URL

4. Click "SAVE"

================================================================================
STEP 5: TEST IN INCOGNITO BROWSER
================================================================================

1. Open a NEW incognito/private browser window

2. Go to your Vercel frontend URL

3. Click "Sign in with Google"

4. ✅ Should work now!

================================================================================
EXAMPLE CONFIGURATION
================================================================================

If your Render backend is: https://student-reg-backend-abc123.onrender.com
And your Vercel frontend is: https://student-registration-form.vercel.app

Then in GOOGLE OAUTH CONSOLE:

Authorized JavaScript origins:
├─ https://student-reg-backend-abc123.onrender.com
├─ https://student-registration-form.vercel.app
├─ http://localhost:3000
└─ http://localhost:5001

Authorized redirect URIs:
├─ https://student-reg-backend-abc123.onrender.com/auth/google/callback
└─ http://localhost:5001/auth/google/callback

And in RENDER environment variables:
GOOGLE_CALLBACK_URL=https://student-reg-backend-abc123.onrender.com/auth/google/callback
FRONTEND_URL=https://student-registration-form.vercel.app
NODE_ENV=production

================================================================================
COMMON MISTAKES
================================================================================

❌ Using http:// instead of https://
❌ Adding trailing slash: /callback/ (wrong) vs /callback (correct)
❌ Copy-pasting with extra spaces
❌ Using .com.com or missing .onrender.com
❌ Not clicking SAVE in Google Console
❌ Testing before waiting 5-10 minutes
❌ Testing in same browser (old cookies cached)

================================================================================
STILL GETTING THE ERROR?
================================================================================

Double-check these:

1. In Google Console, click on your OAuth client ID again
   → Verify the redirect URI is EXACTLY there with no typos

2. In Render dashboard → Environment
   → Verify GOOGLE_CALLBACK_URL matches EXACTLY what's in Google Console

3. Wait the full 10 minutes after saving in Google Console

4. Clear all browser cookies for your site:
   - Chrome: F12 → Application → Storage → Clear site data
   - Then try in fresh incognito window

5. Check your Render logs for errors:
   Render dashboard → Your service → Logs
